EmbroideryAftercare

Aftercare for Eyebrow Embroidery

Fresh embroidery needs gentle care for the first 7–10 days. Keep the area dry, avoid scrubbing, and skip heavy makeup directly on the brows.

A light layer of the aftercare balm we provide helps healing — use sparingly, twice a day. If you swim or exercise, pat dry afterwards rather than rubbing.

Colour may look darker at first; it softens as skin heals. It is completely normal for some light flaking to occur. Message us on WhatsApp if anything feels unusual — we are always happy to check in on our clients' progress.

Why Segar Road Locals Love Korean Misty Eyebrows

Korean misty eyebrow technique uses fine, layered strokes that blend into your natural hair for a soft, powdery finish — not a solid block.

The Ultimate Hydration Facial Routine

Professional facials give skin a reset, but daily habits keep results lasting longer. Drink water regularly, use SPF every morning, and cleanse gently at night.

In Singapore's humidity, lighter moisturisers often work better than heavy creams. If you use actives at home, introduce one product at a time so we can see what your skin tolerates.

Book your next facial when skin feels dull or before a special occasion. At our Bukit Panjang salon, we customize every facial to match exactly what your skin needs that month, leaving you glowing and refreshed.
