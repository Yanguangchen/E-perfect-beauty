Your first eyeliner embroidery visit

25 October 2026 · Evelyn, lead technician

Eyeliner embroidery at E-Perfect Beauty is a fine lash-line, mapped to the eye you have. It is for people who want definition without drawing liner every morning. It is not a thick wing, and it is not a substitute for eyebrow or lip embroidery. The studio is at 550B Segar Road, Singapore 672550, and Evelyn does the mapping herself.

What the result is meant to look like

The published description is subtle lash-line definition, clean and natural, matched to eye shape. If the photos you like are a sharp graphic wing or a line much heavier than your lashes, say that before any pigment is chosen. Evelyn would rather adjust the plan, or tell you it is the wrong treatment, than leave a line you only liked on a reference face.

Brow embroidery and lip embroidery sit on the same menu and solve different problems. Brows are about hair direction and bone structure. Lips are about undertone and an edge that already exists. Eyeliner is the lash line. The first brow visit and the lip embroidery guide are the parallel reads if you are not sure which feature you mean.

What to send before you book

WhatsApp +65 8778 7867 with a straight-on photo of both eyes in daylight, no liner and no mascara if you can manage it. A second photo in your usual liner is useful only as a clue to how much definition you like. Mention contact lenses, a history of styes, and any previous eyeliner or brow embroidery. The price is confirmed on WhatsApp after that look, not printed as a fixed fee on the embroidery section of the pricing page.

On the day

The line is discussed before pigment. You should be able to see where it will sit, how far it travels toward the outer corner, and that it is not a block of colour. Previous embroidery can be assessed first if there is already pigment in the lash line. Do not book the same week as a peel, a strong acid routine around the eyes, or a day you cannot keep the area quiet afterwards.

While it settles

Fresh embroidery looks stronger at the start and softens as the skin settles. Keep the area dry, skip heavy makeup on the line, and do not pick flakes. The aftercare you are given at the appointment is the one to follow. Message the studio if the eye becomes increasingly hot, swollen, or weepy rather than simply tight. How many months the line stays visible is not a number this page publishes. Sun, skincare, and your own skin change it. A touch-up is a later conversation, after Evelyn has seen the healed result.
